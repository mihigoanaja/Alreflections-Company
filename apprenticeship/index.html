Good to know

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations help build a strong team.

Is this a paid apprenticeship?

This is not a directly paid programme and does not promise a salary, stipend, or income. If a project or activity generates benefits, the team will agree in advance how those benefits are shared, taking contributions and project terms into account.

Do I need prior experience?

Not necessarily. We welcome people who are learning, provided they are curious, committed, open to feedback, and ready to contribute at an appropriate level.

Will I work on real projects?

Depending on selection, readiness, and project needs, apprentices may collaborate on practical projects. Responsibilities, supervision, ownership, and any benefit-sharing terms should be clarified before work starts.

Does applying guarantee acceptance?

No. Applications are reviewed according to programme needs and available mentorship capacity. Shortlisted applicants may be invited to a conversation or practical task.

What happens after I submit?

Once this page is connected to Alreflections' application system, your application can be reviewed and the team can contact you using the email or phone number you provided.
