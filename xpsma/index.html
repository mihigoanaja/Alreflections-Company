Questions, answered

Before you request a demo.

What is XPSMA?

XPSMA is a school management system intended to help schools organize administration and school information. Request a demo to confirm the features currently available and how they fit your needs.

Who should enquire about XPSMA?

School owners, headteachers, administrators and other decision-makers who want to review options for improving school operations are welcome to get in touch.

Can I see the system before deciding?

Use the enquiry form to request a walkthrough and discuss the available demonstration options before making a decision.

How much does XPSMA cost?

Pricing is not listed on this page because the appropriate plan and requirements should be confirmed directly. Send an enquiry to ask about pricing, implementation and any applicable requirements.

What should I prepare before the demo?

It helps to know your school's approximate size, the tasks you want to improve and the way you currently manage school information. Please do not submit sensitive student information through this page.
